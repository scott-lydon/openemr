Why clinics adopt this

Pairwise ranking, not list-vomit

Each candidate diagnosis is judged against every other one. No silent biases from list-order, no hallucinated symptoms — every claim cites a FHIR resource in the patient's chart.

Read-only against FHIR

The sidecar pulls Conditions, MedicationRequests, AllergyIntolerances, Observations, Encounters, Procedures, and DocumentReferences. It never writes back. The OpenEMR record is unmodified.

Hash-chained audit log

Every clinician question and every AI answer is appended to a hash-chained audit log with 7-year retention. The chain head is offsite-anchored to AWS S3 Object Lock daily.

BAA chain

OpenAI Enterprise BAA with Zero-Data-Retention. Our own BAA with you. No PHI crosses to Cohere Rerank (re-ranking runs on de-identified candidates only).

Five-minute install

One zip, three clicks in Module Manager, paste the license key and the sidecar URL. INSTALL.md.

Open-source PHP module

The OpenEMR-side glue is GPL-3.0 on GitHub. The diagnostic engine is licensed; you can self-host or use our hosted sidecar.
