Why a pairwise engine, not a list

List-style differential diagnosis tools hide bias in the order of items. A pairwise tournament forces every candidate to be defended against every other one. The answer that survives carries citations.

Typical list-style AI tool

  • Top-ranked answer wins by order bias
  • Confidence numbers are unanchored guesses
  • Often hallucinates symptoms not in the chart
  • No citation back to a FHIR resource
  • Free-form text; auditors hate it

Clinical Co-Pilot pairwise engine

  • Every candidate compared head-to-head
  • Score is derived from win rate, not vibes
  • Each claim cites a Condition, Observation, MedicationRequest, or DocumentReference
  • Output is a structured tournament result an auditor can replay
  • Refuses to answer if it has no evidence; says so explicitly

How it works

The clinician clicks one button on the patient summary. The OpenEMR-side module mints a short-lived task token, the sidecar pulls the chart over FHIR, the pairwise engine runs, and the answer comes back with citations. The clinician's chart is never written to.

AL

Every clinician question and every AI answer is appended to a tamper-evident, hash-chained log. Retained 7 years. Chain head anchored offsite daily.

BA

BAA chain

OpenAI Enterprise BAA with Zero Data Retention. Our BAA with you. Cohere Rerank sees only de-identified candidates. PHI does not leave the chain.

Frequently asked

Short answers below. Full FAQ on faq.html.

Will this write to my patient records?

Diagnostic cross-check and chart-error scan are read-only. The sidecar pulls FHIR resources but does not POST or PUT anything back. Document ingest is the one exception, and it requires the clinician to explicitly confirm each suggested write before it lands in the chart.

Where does the LLM run? Does OpenAI train on my data?

We use OpenAI on the Enterprise tier with the Zero Data Retention policy enabled. OpenAI signs a Business Associate Agreement covering Protected Health Information. No data we send is used to train models. The de-identified rerank step uses Cohere; PHI never reaches it.

Is the OpenEMR module open source?

Yes. The PHP module is GPL-3.0 and lives on GitHub at scott-lydon/openemr. The hosted diagnostic engine is licensed; you can self-host it from the open-source Docker images or use our managed instance.

What happens during an audit?

The hash-chained audit log records every clinician prompt, every AI response, every citation, and the exact FHIR resources fetched. An auditor can replay any session from the log without re-running the LLM. The chain head is anchored offsite to AWS S3 Object Lock daily so tampering is detectable.
