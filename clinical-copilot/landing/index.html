Why this exists

A short version of the story.

Diagnostic error is the single largest source of medical mistake in the United States. The most recent peer-reviewed estimate puts it at about 795,000 Americans permanently disabled or killed every year from misdiagnosis, and roughly 12 million more harmed in outpatient care (Newman-Toker et al, BMJ Quality and Safety, 2024; Singh et al, BMJ Quality and Safety, 2014).

Most of those cases are not exotic. They are everyday charts where one fact contradicted another and nobody caught it. A med marked active that the latest diagnosis no longer supports. An allergy hiding three encounters back. A lab value that should have changed the workup.

Clinical Co-Pilot is a second set of eyes that does not get tired, does not get distracted, and never skips a row. It does not replace the clinician. It just makes sure no row goes unread.

What is in the module

Three checkers. The first two ship today. The third is in active development.

DX

Diagnostic Checker LIVE

Click one button on the patient summary. The Co-Pilot reads Conditions, Observations, MedicationRequests, AllergyIntolerances, Encounters, Procedures, and DocumentReferences over FHIR, then returns a ranked list of candidate diagnoses, each backed by a citation into the chart. If it cannot cite, it does not claim.

CE

Chart Error Checker LIVE

Detects internal contradictions across the chart: a med recorded as active that the latest diagnosis no longer supports, units that disagree across encounters, an allergy buried in an old DocumentReference that contraindicates an active order. The output is a list of specific fields with the offending citations.

CI

Contraindication Checker COMING SOON

Cross-references medications against allergies, against existing conditions, and against each other for known interactions. Returns each flag with the source row in the chart and a link to the underlying interaction database. In active development; ship date depends on the underlying drug-interaction data licence.

How it works

The clinician clicks one button on the patient summary. The OpenEMR-side module mints a short-lived task token, the sidecar pulls the chart over FHIR, the engine runs, and the answer comes back with citations. The chart is never written to.

What makes it safe to put in front of patients

Six non-negotiables baked into the module.

RO

Read-only against the chart

The sidecar holds an OAuth access token scoped to read on the standard FHIR resource set. It cannot POST or PUT. The chart is never modified by an answer.

CT

Citations required

Every claim ships with the source FHIR resource. No citation, no claim. The engine refuses to answer a question it cannot back up from the chart.

AL

Hash-chained audit log

Every prompt, every answer, every citation goes into a tamper-evident log. Retained seven years. Chain head anchored offsite daily.

BA

BAA chain

OpenAI Enterprise BAA with Zero Data Retention. Our BAA with you. Cohere Rerank sees only de-identified candidates. PHI never leaves the chain.

IG

Injection guard

The chat router rejects prompt-injection patterns embedded in patient data and caps the per-session turn count so a stuck client cannot loop the LLM.

CL

Clinician owns the decision

Clinical Co-Pilot does not prescribe, order, or modify the chart. It surfaces what it found, with citations. The clinician decides what to do.

Frequently asked

Short answers below. Full FAQ on faq.html.

Will this write to my patient records?

The Diagnostic Checker and Chart Error Checker are strictly read-only. The sidecar pulls FHIR resources but does not POST or PUT anything back. Document ingest (a separate Pro-tier flow) is the one exception, and it requires the clinician to explicitly confirm each suggested write before it lands.

Where does the LLM run? Does OpenAI train on my data?

We use OpenAI on the Enterprise tier with the Zero Data Retention policy enabled. OpenAI signs a Business Associate Agreement covering Protected Health Information. No data we send is used to train models. The de-identified rerank step uses Cohere; PHI never reaches it.

Is the OpenEMR module open source?

Yes. The PHP module is GPL-3.0 and lives on GitHub at scott-lydon/openemr. The hosted diagnostic engine is licensed; you can self-host from the open-source Docker images or use our managed instance.

What happens during an audit?

The hash-chained audit log records every clinician prompt, every AI response, every citation, and the exact FHIR resources fetched. An auditor can replay any session from the log without re-running the LLM. The chain head is anchored offsite to AWS S3 Object Lock daily so tampering is detectable.
