Frequently asked about pricing

What counts as a clinician?

Anyone who actually clicks the Co-Pilot button on a chart in a given month. Staff who never touch the Co-Pilot UI do not count. The license check is on the OpenEMR user ID of the person who initiated the request.

What does the 14-day trial include?

Pro tier for fourteen days on up to three clinicians. No credit card. At day fourteen the trial flips off; nothing is charged unless you explicitly subscribe in the Stripe portal.

How do I cancel?

Sign in to the Stripe customer portal we email you on signup and click Cancel subscription. The Co-Pilot stops accepting prompts at the end of the current billing period. The audit log is retained for the full seven years regardless of subscription status, per HIPAA.

Volume discount?

Above twenty clinicians on Pro, email us for a quote. Enterprise tier is always priced for the customer's scale and on-prem requirements.

Do you offer a free tier?

There is no perpetual free tier today; the LLM and infrastructure costs do not allow for one. The 14-day Pro trial is the closest path to evaluating without a contract.
